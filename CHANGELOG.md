# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-09)

The first public version. It gathers the previews that went to testers, in order. No buddies are included: a first run starts on the Manager's empty state, with Import and New buddy.

### The web Maker
- Make a buddy from one image or a full set of pose frames, with Shimeji-style poses, lines for what she says, and a prompt kit for image AIs.
- Export a standalone page with your buddies on it, which opens from a file and needs no network.

### Preview 1: on the desktop
- One world across every monitor. Buddies walk, climb, hang, nap and talk, and can be dragged and thrown between screens.
- A tray icon to pause, hide or quit.

### Preview 2: the Manager
- A Manager window, opened from the tray: call and dismiss buddies, call more copies, import and export `.buddy` files, bring in a Shimeji folder, and delete.
- Failures are said in plain words.

### Preview 3: the sticker sheet
- The Manager in its sticker look, with a dotted cut line round each buddy who's out on the desktop.
- Find, delete with a confirm, a light theme, and a title bar of its own.

### Preview 4: your windows
- Buddies use your real windows: they walk along the tops, climb the sides, hang underneath and jump from window to window. Move a window and they ride along; close it and they fall.
- The app reads only which windows are open, where, and which app each belongs to. Never titles or contents.
- Settings can turn this off, or keep buddies off one app.

### Preview 5: the Maker in the app
- Make a new buddy or change one, in the same sticker look. It saves as you go.

### Preview 6: bring them back, and Lift
- Bring everyone here, in the tray. Bring here, Give a treat and Chase my mouse for each buddy in the Manager.
- Lift in the Maker: how far she stands off the floor, out from walls and down from ceilings, and per pose on the floor.
- Much faster saves for big buddies, and a safer Close for the Maker.

### Preview 7: smoother moves
- Opening a file for a buddy you already have asks first: Replace, or Keep both.
- New optional poses: Sit down, Fall asleep, a way up from each kind of fall, and Roll.
- Gestures finish their loop before she moves on. Shake her while you hold her and she struggles free.
- A setting, off unless you turn it on, lets other apps on your PC ask buddies to wave or say a line, with the Manager asking you first for each app.

### Preview 8: right-click
- Right-click a buddy for her actions, the same list as her button in the Manager. Arrow keys and Enter work in it.
- A buddy sunk into the floor with Lift is drawn over the taskbar instead of being cut off.
- Long speech bubbles wrap onto more lines, and a bubble near the screen's edge moves in to stay whole.
